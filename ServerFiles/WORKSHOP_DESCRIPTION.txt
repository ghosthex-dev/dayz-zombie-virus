[h1]Zombie Virus (Z-Strain Infection & Playable Zombie)[/h1]

Every strike from an infected carries a risk of transmission with the deadly [b]Z-strain virus[/b]. Without treatment, the pathogen takes hold, breaking your body down across four deteriorating stages. If left untreated, you succumb to the virus—and [b]take direct control of the newly risen infected![/b]

100% standalone and vanilla-compatible. No third-party frameworks required.

[hr][/hr]

[h2]Features[/h2]

[b]Transmission & Progression (Default: 60 min incubation)[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Infection Chance:[/b] 8% on damaging hits; 25% if the hit causes bleeding. Blocking reduces chance by 75%.
[*] [b]Status Tray HUD:[/b] Custom biohazard "Z" icon in the HUD tray tracks your stage (Grey = Incubation, Yellow = Fever, Orange = Severe Sickness, Red = Terminal).
[*] [b]Symptoms:[/b] Burning wounds, fever, coughing, nausea, vomiting fits, manic laughter, and terminal health drain.
[*] [b]Death at Stage 2+:[/b] Dying of gunfire, cold, or wounds while infected still reanimates your corpse. Gear remains lootable on the ground.
[/list]

[b]Playable Feral Zombie Mode (Enabled by default)[/b]
When your humanity is lost, you take physical control of the infected!
[list]
[*] [b]Locked 3rd-Person Camera:[/b] Smooth third-person perspective centered behind your driven zombie model.
[*] [b]Infected Creature Physics:[/b] Responsive movement with unlimited runner sprint stamina.
[*] [b]Lethal Claw Attacks:[/b] Left-click to execute standing swipes or running leap attacks. Strikes deal heavy damage, cause severe bleeding, and guarantee 100% infection transmission to living victims.
[*] [b]Feed to Heal:[/b] Devour raw animal meat, guts, or survivor flesh to heal your decaying body.
[*] [b]Horde Camouflage:[/b] Wild AI infected recognize you as part of the horde and ignore you.
[*] [b]Feral Decay:[/b] Feral body lasts 15 minutes by default (fully configurable or unlimited).
[*] [i]Prefer classic AI rise? Toggle [code]"PlayableZombie": false[/code] to have players die and an AI zombie spawn instead.[/i]
[/list]

[b]Cure & Prevention[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Z-Strain Antiviral:[/b] Rare military/medical autoinjector with a distinctive green label. Clears the virus at any stage, removes all symptoms, and grants [b]30 minutes of complete bite immunity[/b].
[*] [b]Vaccine Function:[/b] Healthy players can inject it preventatively for 30 minutes of bite immunity.
[*] [b]Tetracycline:[/b] Slows virus progression by 50% while active.
[/list]

[h2]Server Installation[/h2]

[olist]
[*] Copy [code]@ZombieVirus[/code] to your server root and [code]@ZombieVirus/keys/GhostHex.bikey[/code] to your [code]keys/[/code] folder.
[*] Add [code]-mod=@ZombieVirus[/code] to your server startup line.
[*] Copy [code]ServerFiles/zv_types.xml[/code] to your mission folder (e.g. [code]mpmissions/dayzOffline.chernarusplus/zombievirus/zv_types.xml[/code]) and register it in [code]cfgeconomycore.xml[/code]:
[code]
<ce folder="zombievirus">
    <file name="zv_types.xml" type="types" />
</ce>
[/code]
[*] Start the server once to generate [code]<server_profile>/ZombieVirus/settings.json[/code].
[/olist]

[h2]Configuration (settings.json)[/h2]

Located at [code]<server_profile>/ZombieVirus/settings.json[/code]:

[list]
[*] [b]InfectionChance (0.08):[/b] Base infection chance (0.0 to 1.0) on damaging hits.
[*] [b]InfectionChanceIfWounded (0.25):[/b] Infection chance if the hit causes bleeding.
[*] [b]BlockedHitMultiplier (0.25):[/b] Multiplier when hit while blocking.
[*] [b]IncubationMinutes (60.0):[/b] Total online minutes until turned.
[*] [b]Stage1At / Stage2At / Stage3At (0.25 / 0.5 / 0.8):[/b] Incubation progress thresholds for stages.
[*] [b]AntibioticsSpeedMultiplier (0.5):[/b] Progression speed while Tetracycline is active.
[*] [b]CureImmunityMinutes (30.0):[/b] Minutes of immunity granted by Z-Strain Antiviral.
[*] [b]Stage3HealthDrainPerMinute (4.0):[/b] Health drain per minute in terminal Stage 3.
[*] [b]TurnDelaySeconds (5.0):[/b] Seconds before rising as an infected.
[*] [b]RiseOnDeathMinStage (2):[/b] Minimum stage where dying of other causes still reanimates (4 = disable).
[*] [b]DeleteBodyOnTurn (false):[/b] If true, despawns corpse/gear upon turning.
[*] [b]NotifyPlayer (true):[/b] Enables stage notification popups.
[*] [b]PlayableZombie (true):[/b] Enables playable feral zombie mode upon turning.
[*] [b]PlayableZombieDurationMinutes (15.0):[/b] Feral duration before body collapses (0 = unlimited).
[*] [b]PlayableZombieIgnoredByAI (true):[/b] Wild AI infected ignore turned players.
[*] [b]PlayableZombieInfiniteStamina (true):[/b] Unlimited sprint stamina while feral.
[*] [b]PlayableZombieDamageMultiplier (2.0):[/b] Melee attack damage multiplier against survivors.
[*] [b]PlayableZombieHealthDrainPerMinute (2.0):[/b] Health decay per minute while feral.
[*] [b]PlayableZombieHealthGainOnFeed (25.0):[/b] Health gained from consuming meat/flesh.
[*] [b]MaleZombies / FemaleZombies:[/b] Configurable array of zombie class names spawned on turn.
[/list]

[b]Safe Persistence:[/b] Saved player infections are tracked in [code]ZombieVirus/players.json[/code] (saved every 30s and on disconnect). It never touches vanilla player character database tables and will never corrupt player saves.
