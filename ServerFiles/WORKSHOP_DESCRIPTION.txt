[h1]Zombie Virus (Z-Strain Infection)[/h1]

A zombie hit is no longer just a loss of health—it is a race against time. Every strike from an infected carries a risk of transmission with the deadly [b]Z-strain virus[/b]. Without treatment, the pathogen takes hold, breaking your body down across four deteriorating stages until you collapse... only to rise again as one of them.

[hr][/hr]

[h2]Survivors' Guide: How It Works[/h2]

[h3]Infection & Transmission[/h3]
[list]
[*] [b]Damaging Hits:[/b] Any unblocked zombie hit that inflicts health or blood damage has an [b]8% chance[/b] to infect.
[*] [b]Bleeding Wounds:[/b] If the hit opens a bleeding wound, the transmission chance spikes to [b]25%[/b].
[*] [b]Blocking:[/b] Hits absorbed while blocking reduce the transmission chance to a quarter ([b]2%[/b] or [b]6.25%[/b] if wounded). Blocked light attacks deal no damage and cannot infect.
[/list]

[h3]Infection Stages (Default: 60 Online Minutes)[/h3]
Infection progress only ticks while you are [b]logged in[/b] (it pauses safely on logout). A custom biohazard icon appears in your status icon tray (bottom right):

[list]
[*] [b]Stage 0 — Incubation (0% – 25%)[/b] [i][Grey Icon][/i]
No physical symptoms. You feel a burning sensation at the wound: [i]"The bite burns."[/i]
[*] [b]Stage 1 — Fever (25% – 50%)[/b] [i][Yellow Icon][/i]
Fever sets in. Mild coughing and sneezing begin. [i]"You feel feverish."[/i]
[*] [b]Stage 2 — Severe (50% – 80%)[/b] [i][Orange Icon][/i]
Severe sickness. Periodic coughing, violent vomiting fits (emptying your stomach contents), and muscle pain. [i]"Your veins burn."[/i]
[*] [b]Stage 3 — Terminal (80% – 100%)[/b] [i][Red Icon][/i]
Critical condition. Heavy agony, fever blur vision, uncontrollable shaking, manic laughter, and steady health drain (-4 HP/min). [i]"You are losing yourself."[/i]
[*] [b]Turned (100%) — Feral Zombie Transformation[/b]
By default, you do not simply die—you [b]become the zombie[/b]! Take direct control of an infected creature and hunt the living:
[list]
[*] [b]Third-Person Camera:[/b] Perspective locks behind the driven zombie model with full mouse look.
[*] [b]True Infected Movement:[/b] Responsive creature movement with infinite sprint stamina (runner zombie).
[*] [b]Lethal Claw Attacks:[/b] Left-click to perform standing swipes or running leap attacks that deal heavy damage, induce severe bleeding, and guarantee 100% infection transmission to victims.
[*] [b]Feeding & Survival:[/b] Consume raw meat, guts, or survivor flesh to heal your decaying body.
[*] [b]Horde Camouflage:[/b] Wild AI infected recognize you as one of their own and ignore you.
[*] [b]Configurable:[/b] Server owners can set feral duration (default: 15 min), toggle horde camouflage, tune damage, or disable playable mode ([code]"PlayableZombie": false[/code]) to have players die and rise as AI instead.
[/list]
[/list]

[h3]Death, Loot, & Rising[/h3]
[list]
[*] [b]Death at Stage 2 or 3:[/b] If an infected survivor is killed by bullets, wildlife, or starvation while at Stage 2 or higher, the virus still reanimates their corpse.
[*] [b]Corpse & Gear Recovery:[/b] By default, the survivor's corpse remains on the ground next to the newly risen zombie with all gear intact, allowing teammates to avenge them and recover their loot.
[/list]

[h3]Treatment & Prevention[/h3]
[list]
[*] [b]Tetracycline / Antibiotics:[/b] Does not cure the Z-strain, but slows the progression speed by [b]50%[/b] while the antibiotic buff is active.
[*] [b]Z-Strain Antiviral:[/b] An experimental autoinjector with a distinctive green label.
[list]
[*] [b]Full Cure:[/b] Completely cures the virus at any stage, removes all symptoms, and grants [b]30 minutes of total immunity[/b].
[*] [b]Vaccine:[/b] Injected while healthy, it acts as a preventative vaccine providing 30 minutes of immunity against bites.
[*] Can be self-administered or injected into other players.
[*] Spawns as rare medical loot in hospitals and military medical tents.
[/list]
[/list]

[h2]Server Owners: Installation Guide[/h2]

[b]Zombie Virus[/b] is 100% standalone and vanilla-compatible. It requires no external frameworks or third-party mods.

[h3]Step 1: Install Mod & Key[/h3]
[olist]
[*] Copy the [code]@ZombieVirus[/code] folder to your server root.
[*] Copy the public key [code]@ZombieVirus/keys/GhostHex.bikey[/code] into your server's root [code]keys/[/code] directory.
[*] Add [code]-mod=@ZombieVirus[/code] to your server launch parameters.
[/olist]

[h3]Step 2: Add Antiviral Loot Spawns (Central Economy)[/h3]
[olist]
[*] Copy [code]zv_types.xml[/code] (located inside [code]ServerFiles/[/code]) into your mission folder, for example:
[code]mpmissions/dayzOffline.chernarusplus/zombievirus/zv_types.xml[/code]
[*] Register it in [code]cfgeconomycore.xml[/code] right before [code]</economycore>[/code]:
[code]
<ce folder="zombievirus">
    <file name="zv_types.xml" type="types" />
</ce>
[/code]
[i](Alternatively, you can paste the [code]<type name="ZV_Antiviral">[/code] block directly into your mission's [code]db/types.xml[/code]).[/i]
[/olist]

[h3]Step 3: First Startup[/h3]
Start the server once. The mod will automatically create its default configuration file at:
[code]<server_profile>/ZombieVirus/settings.json[/code]

[h3]Setting Explanations[/h3]
[list]
[*] [b]InfectionChance (Default: 0.08):[/b] Base chance (0.0 to 1.0) for any damaging zombie hit to infect.
[*] [b]InfectionChanceIfWounded (Default: 0.25):[/b] Chance when that hit also opens a bleeding wound.
[*] [b]BlockedHitMultiplier (Default: 0.25):[/b] Multiplier applied when the player takes damage while in block stance.
[*] [b]IncubationMinutes (Default: 60.0):[/b] Total online minutes from initial infection to turning.
[*] [b]Stage1At / Stage2At / Stage3At (Defaults: 0.25 / 0.5 / 0.8):[/b] Threshold fractions of incubation where each stage triggers.
[*] [b]AntibioticsSpeedMultiplier (Default: 0.5):[/b] Progress speed multiplier while Tetracycline is active (0.5 = progresses at half speed; 1.0 = normal speed).
[*] [b]CureImmunityMinutes (Default: 30.0):[/b] Minutes of bite immunity granted after taking a Z-Strain Antiviral.
[*] [b]Stage3HealthDrainPerMinute (Default: 4.0):[/b] Health points lost per minute during terminal Stage 3 (players have 100 HP).
[*] [b]TurnDelaySeconds (Default: 5.0):[/b] Seconds between death and the new zombie standing up.
[*] [b]RiseOnDeathMinStage (Default: 2):[/b] Minimum stage (0 to 3) where dying of other causes (gunfire, cold, etc.) still spawns a zombie (set to 4 to disable).
[*] [b]DeleteBodyOnTurn (Default: false):[/b] When true, the dead survivor's corpse and gear despawn when the zombie rises. When false, the corpse stays lootable.
[*] [b]NotifyPlayer (Default: true):[/b] Displays stage notification popups to the player.
[*] [b]PlayableZombie (Default: true):[/b] Enables playable feral zombie mode upon turning. If false, player dies and an AI zombie rises.
[*] [b]PlayableZombieDurationMinutes (Default: 15.0):[/b] Survival duration for feral zombie body in minutes before expiring (0 = unlimited).
[*] [b]PlayableZombieIgnoredByAI (Default: true):[/b] Wild AI zombies ignore player-controlled zombies (horde camouflage).
[*] [b]PlayableZombieInfiniteStamina (Default: true):[/b] Feral zombie has unlimited sprint stamina (runner zombie).
[*] [b]PlayableZombieDamageMultiplier (Default: 2.0):[/b] Melee attack damage multiplier against living survivors.
[*] [b]PlayableZombieHealthDrainPerMinute (Default: 2.0):[/b] Passive health decay per minute while in feral form.
[*] [b]PlayableZombieHealthGainOnFeed (Default: 25.0):[/b] Health restored when feeding on raw meat or survivor flesh.
[*] [b]MaleZombies / FemaleZombies:[/b] Configurable array of zombie class names spawned when male or female survivors turn.
[/list]

[h3]Safe Persistence (No Character Corruption)[/h3]
Active infections are stored in [code]<server_profile>/ZombieVirus/players.json[/code]. State is saved every 30 seconds, on logout, and during server shutdown. Because data is stored in its own dedicated JSON file rather than vanilla character database tables, adding, updating, or removing this mod will [b]never corrupt player characters or save files[/b].
